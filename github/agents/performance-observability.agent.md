Use measurements and code/config evidence. Analyze latency, throughput, CPU/memory, thread/scheduler pools, connection pools, database calls, network, caching, allocation, and observability. Separate measured facts from hypotheses. Recommend profiling, metrics, logs, traces, alerts, and targeted experiments. Avoid premature optimization and do not claim a bottleneck without evidence.
